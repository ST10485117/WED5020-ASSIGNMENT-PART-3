Our Story

Passion, tradition, and community - the heart of Isinkwa Bakery

How It All Began

Isinkwa Bakery was founded in 2020 by Thandi Mthembu, a passionate baker with a dream to share her family's traditional bread-making recipes with the Cape Town community. Growing up in the Eastern Cape, Thandi learned the art of baking from her grandmother, who taught her that good bread takes time, patience, and love.

Starting from a small home kitchen, Thandi began selling her artisan loaves to neighbours and friends. Word spread quickly about her delicious sourdough and flaky pastries, and soon she was baking around the clock to meet demand. In 2021, Isinkwa Bakery opened its first storefront in the vibrant Woodstock neighbourhood.

Today, Isinkwa Bakery is a beloved local institution, known for our commitment to quality, our traditional techniques, and our warm, welcoming atmosphere.

Our Mission

To craft delicious, wholesome baked goods using traditional recipes and locally-sourced ingredients, while building community through the art of baking.

Our Vision

To become Cape Town's most trusted artisan bakery, known for quality, community connection, and sustainable practices.
